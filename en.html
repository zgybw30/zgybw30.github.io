ABOUT

About Harmony

Harmony is a local AI visual production studio built by independent developer zgybw. It brings image editing, layers, local AI image generation and batch pipelines into one desktop app, made for photo studios, workshops and short-video creators.

It runs the local route on purpose: models and media live on your own computer and it works fully offline. Nothing passes through a third-party server — client photos never leave your shop or your machine.

FAQ

Questions before you buy

Can my PC run it?

Local AI image generation leans on your GPU's VRAM. Minimum RTX 3060 12GB, RTX 3090-class recommended, 16GB RAM or more suggested. If your machine is below the minimum, please don't buy.

Where do I get the model weights?

The app bundles no model weights — you supply your own. The GGUF weights for Qwen-Image-2.1 are free to download from ModelScope; point the app's Settings at the file. Model licenses stay with their providers; please follow those terms.

Trial vs. Pro — what's the difference?

You get 7 days with every feature. When the trial ends, the free version disables generation and exports carry a watermark. Importing a license restores everything immediately, watermark-free.

Does activation need internet or an account?

No. Your license is an offline signed file: import it in the app and activation is complete. No registration, no machine account, no server check-in.

Windows SmartScreen is blocking the app — what now?

The app is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may show a blue warning. Click “More info” → “Run anyway”. Only download from this site or official channels, never from third-party mirrors.

How many computers can I use it on?

One license binds to one computer: when you order, send me your email and the machine code shown in the app (System → License). If you replace your PC or reinstall Windows, contact me for a free rebind with the new machine code — the old license is revoked at the same time.

What about refunds?

Harmony is a digital product, and it comes with a 7-day full-feature free trial — please try it first and make sure it fits your PC and your needs. Because a delivered digital licence cannot be taken back, purchases are non-refundable.
